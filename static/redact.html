      Info

      How to use it

      Upload a PDF, then mark what to remove: type a name or phrase and click Mark, use a preset (SSNs, emails, phone numbers), or drag a box over any area. Review the black marks on each page and click any mark to drop it. Click Apply redactions. The tool builds the redacted file, re-opens it, checks that nothing marked can still be found, and then lets you download it.

      What "true redaction" means here

      • Marked text is deleted from the PDF's content, not just covered, so it can't be copied, searched, or recovered by removing the box.
      • Image pixels under a box are blanked, and vector drawings fully covered by a box (for example, signatures) are removed.
      • The file is rewritten from scratch, so earlier saved revisions and unused objects holding the original content are dropped.

      Privacy

      Redaction runs in your browser using a PDF engine compiled to WebAssembly. The engine's code is downloaded from the jsDelivr CDN at a fixed version; your document is never uploaded anywhere.

      Limitations

      • Search only finds text the PDF actually contains. Scanned pages need boxes drawn by hand (OCR is on the roadmap), and text baked into images, such as logos and screenshots, won't be found by search.
      • A search for a name won't catch variations ("J. Smith", "Smith's", initials). Always review every page before applying.
      • Comments, form fields, and stamps are flattened into the page, so the output is no longer a fillable form.
      • The automatic check confirms that marked text was removed. It does not prove that nothing sensitive was missed.

      Engines

      Choose the engine with the Engine toggle at the top. Switching reopens the document and keeps your marks. After applying, Cross-check re-opens the output with the other engine and runs its leak check as well.

      • MuPDF: MuPDF.js by Artifex Software, licensed under the GNU AGPL v3. Source: github.com/ArtifexSoftware/mupdf.js.
      • PDFium: Google's PDFium (Apache-2.0) as built by EmbedPDF (MIT), plus pdf-lib (MIT) to clean up document structure. Known difference: text inside a reusable page fragment (form XObject) that touches a redaction box can be removed in full rather than just the marked words.
